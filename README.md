# news-image-20261124 - The World in One Day

An entry for make.fun's [News Image 20261124](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261124) bounty.

The entry is a single vintage broadsheet front page, masthead "THE WORLD IN ONE DAY", dated Tuesday 24 November 2026, set in black and white newspaper style. Its six headlines:

- COP31 ends with no deal on fossil fuels in Antalya
- Ukraine sinks a Russian warship off Libya; drone strike on the Sparta II in the Gulf of Finland
- Fire at a Banten candy factory kills 24
- ICJ rules Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya
- Alcaraz beats Zverev in Turin for his first ATP Finals title
- Migrant boat capsizes off Senegal, dozens missing

`main.py` validates the shipped `entry.png` (PNG signature, full chunk walk with CRC checks, the 1 MiB and 1024x1024 caps) and copies it byte for byte to `/output/entry.png`.
